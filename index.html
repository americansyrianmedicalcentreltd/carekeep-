1. About this policy

This policy explains how the CareKeep Android application ("CareKeep", "the app", "we") handles information. CareKeep is a home maintenance tracker that helps you record appliances, warranties and repair history.

2. Information you enter

To use the app you may type details such as:

  • Appliance names, categories, brands, models and serial numbers
  • Purchase dates, purchase prices and warranty dates
  • Service and repair dates, costs, technician names and phone numbers, and notes
  • Your preferences: theme, reminder window and currency symbol

This information is saved only in the app's private storage on your device (a local database and local settings). It is never sent to us or to any server.

3. Information we do not collect

Personal data (name, email, phone of the user)Not collected
LocationNot collected
Contacts, photos, files, microphone, cameraNot accessed
Device identifiers and advertising IDNot collected
Usage analytics and crash reportingNone included
AdvertisingNone
Accounts or sign-inNone

4. Permissions

CareKeep declares no Android permissions. It does not use the internet, notifications, location, storage access, camera or contacts. Reminders are shown inside the app dashboard only.

5. Storage and security

Your data is kept in the app's sandboxed storage, which other apps cannot normally read. Because nothing leaves the device, its safety depends on your device's own protections, such as a screen lock and system updates. If you use Android's device backup or a device transfer, the operating system may include app data in that backup under your own Google or device settings. We do not receive or control those backups.

6. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell, rent, trade or share your information. The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking libraries.

7. Your control

  • Edit or delete any appliance or maintenance record inside the app at any time.
  • Use Settings → Delete all data to remove everything stored by the app.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its locally stored data from your device.

Since we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to access, export or erase on a server.

8. Children

CareKeep is a general utility app and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, for example if a future version adds a feature that uses a permission or a network connection, this policy will be updated and the effective date above will change. Please review it from time to time.
